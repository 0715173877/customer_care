 SECTION 1: STANDARD COMMAND FORMAT (Confidence: 95%)
======================================================================
 Use these for manually sending payment instructions.

 EXACT RULES:
   • Must start with "SEND" or "TRANSFER" (case-insensitive)
   • Amount can be plain (200000) or with commas (50,000)
   • Currency can be: TZS, USD, EUR, GBP, KES, UGX, RWF, BIF
   • From/To types: BANK, MOMO, or INTL
   • REF is optional — use REF= or REF:

──── Template ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 SEND <AMOUNT> <CURRENCY> FROM <TYPE> <ACCOUNT> TO <TYPE> <ACCOUNT> REF=<REFERENCE>
 TRANSFER AMOUNT=<AMOUNT> <CURRENCY> FROM <TYPE> <ACCOUNT> TO <TYPE> <ACCOUNT>
